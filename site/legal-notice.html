Data origin

The data is automatically extracted from the Release Notes of Cisco IOS, IOS XE, IOS XR, NX-OS, Secure Firewall ASA and Secure Firewall Threat Defense, published by Cisco Systems on cisco.com. The notes followed are discovered from the "Release Notes" page of each product series. Each bug links to the page of the official note mentioning it, and to its entry in Cisco's Bug Search Tool (Cisco account required). Security advisories are left out.

The end of maintenance and end of support dates of the IOS XE branches come from endoflife.date, which lists them from Cisco's bulletins. Those of NX-OS come from the "Cisco NX-OS Software Lifecycle Support Statement", those of FTD and the last FTD release of each Firepower platform from the "Cisco Secure Firewall Threat Defense Compatibility Guide", those of IOS XR and ASA from Cisco's "End-of-Sale and End-of-Life" announcements (cisco.com). A branch Cisco has not announced yet stays supported, without a date. For IOS, without a published table, the site calls a branch "active" when one of its notes was revised in the last twelve months. In the Hardware tab, the end of life comes from Cisco's "End-of-Sale and End-of-Life" bulletins, listed on the "EoS/EoL notices" page of each series followed (cisco.com).
